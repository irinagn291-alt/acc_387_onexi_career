# OneXI Career

## What it is

OneXI Career is an offline, single-player football career simulation for people who want to develop a fictional player through training, simulated fixtures and club moves. Players choose a position and match approach, then follow their results, league standings, trophies and career milestones across seasons.

## Launch and onboarding

The quoted app copy throughout this brief is from the English interface.

1. On a fresh launch, the app opens directly to “New career”. It shows “OneXI Career”, the heading “Your first step to greatness.” and the introduction “Create a footballer. Train your strengths, play every fixture, and write your own club story.”
2. Under “Player name”, the empty text field shows “e.g. Alex Rivera”. Use the native stepper beside “Shirt number” to decrease or increase the initial “09”. The range is 1–99.
3. Under “Choose your position”, tap one of three cards. “Striker” is selected by default and says “Lead the line. Finishing starts at 54.” The alternatives are “Midfielder”, with “Build the play. Passing starts at 54.”, and “Defender”, with “Protect the team. Defending starts at 54.”
4. Enter a name to enable “Begin career”. The footer reads “Entirely offline. Free to play. Your career stays on this device.” Tapping “Begin career” saves the player and opens “Career” at Lumen Academy in “SEASON 1”. The other tabs are “Training”, “Clubs” and “Legacy”.
5. On a normal relaunch with an active saved player, the app opens “Career” for that player with their saved progress. “New career” also opens from “Settings”, where an additional “Cancel” button returns to “Settings”.

## Features

- “New career” creates a named “Striker”, “Midfielder” or “Defender” with a chosen “Shirt number”. “Saved careers” lets players keep, switch between and delete up to four independent careers.
- “Match approach” offers “Balanced”, “Attack” and “Defend”. “Simulate match” generates the result using skills, fitness, team strength, home advantage and approach. The rest of the league plays its round at the same time.
- “Match report”, “From the touchline” and “Match journal” show scores, personal contributions, match ratings and event summaries. “Season pulse” shows season progress, standings and recent form.
- “Training” lets players earn and spend “Practice points” on five skills and manage “Fitness” through “Recovery session · +30 fitness”.
- “Fixtures & table” shows the standings and the full fourteen-round home and away schedule.
- “Clubs” tracks five tiers and season-end offers earned through “Overall”, career “Matches” and “Reputation”. Players can use “Join this club” to advance or “Stay & start next season” to continue at the same club.
- “Legacy” tracks career totals, five “Legend milestones”, four types of trophy in “Trophy cabinet”, and completed seasons in “Season archive”.
- “Career reminders” provides optional notifications for the upcoming match and the projected season finish. “Career at a glance” provides Home Screen and Lock Screen views of the active career.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “Begin career” stays disabled with an empty or whitespace-only “Player name”. Enter a name containing a visible character. The saved name is trimmed and limited to 30 characters. “e.g. Alex Rivera” is placeholder text.
- “New career” in “Saved careers” is disabled at four careers. Use a player row to resume a career or confirm “Delete” to free a slot. Deleting the active career selects the first remaining career. Deleting the last one returns to “New career”.
- “Train” is disabled when the displayed points cost exceeds available “Practice points”, fitness is below six, the skill shows “Skill mastered”, or the season is complete. Play a fixture for more points, use an available recovery session for fitness, pick a skill below 99, or start the next season as applicable. A match earns four practice points, or six for a rating of at least 8.
- “Recovery session · +30 fitness” is disabled at full fitness, after recovery has already been used since the last match, or after the season has ended. Playing the next match reopens the recovery opportunity if fitness is below full. Starting the next season restores full fitness.
- “Simulate match” can be used immediately at any fitness level, including before the reminder date. “Continue career” and “Done” return from the report to “Career”. Each simulation advances one round. After round 14, use “Review club path” or the “Clubs” tab to advance the season.
- “Join this club” only appears after round 14, for the next tier, when all three listed thresholds are met. “Stay & start next season” is available after every completed season, including at “Summit United”. A new season resets the table, round, season stats and fitness. Player skills, practice points, career totals, trophies and archived seasons carry forward.
- “Your form begins with your first match.” and “Play your first match to start your story.” clear after the first simulation. “LAST FIVE MATCHES” rolls forward across seasons, while the average rating starts over each season. “Match journal” shows the eight most recent results even as its total played count grows beyond eight.
- “Complete your first season to leave a mark.” clears after the fourteenth fixture. “Win the league, score 10 goals, make 8 assists or keep 5 clean sheets in one season.” remains until at least one trophy is awarded at season end.
- “Next match reminder” and “Save reminder date” are disabled while the season is complete. Start the next season to use them again. If the system has blocked notifications, enable them in iOS Settings and turn on “Career reminders”. Playing a fixture replaces the saved next-match date with one day ahead. The season reminder estimates the finish from the remaining fixtures and the next-match date.
- Error alerts are titled “Notice” and close with “OK”:
  - “The saved career file could not be read. It has not been changed. Restart the app before creating a new career if you want to preserve it.”
  - “Progress could not be saved. Check available device storage and try again.”
  - “The reminder could not be scheduled. Your career is still playable.”

## Starter content and resume

There is no pre-created player, sample result, archived season or trophy. A new player starts at “Lumen Academy” in “Academy” with eight “Practice points”, 100% “Fitness” and an “Overall” of 46. The chosen position’s specialty starts at 54, “Pace” starts at 48, and the remaining skills start at 44. The first opponent is “Aurora SC”. The seven fictional rivals are “Cedar Rovers”, “Vale Athletic”, “Orion FC”, “Riverside”, “Bluehaven”, “Atlas Town” and “Aurora SC”.

Creating a player, playing a match, training, recovering, moving clubs and starting a season save progress as they happen. On relaunch, the active player resumes from the saved season and round. A match already simulated can be reopened from “Match journal”. Unsubmitted player creation fields are temporary.

## Permissions

Notifications are the only permission requested. The iOS notification prompt is first triggered when the player turns on “Career reminders” in “Settings”. The app requests alerts and sound. iOS supplies the prompt wording and there is no app-defined usage-description string.

The two notification titles are “Next career match” and “Career season reminder”. The match message inserts the player name and opponent, for example “Alex Rivera, your match against Aurora SC is ready whenever you are.” The season message reads “Return to your career and play your remaining fixtures. Matches only advance when you play.”

## Absent

- Login or accounts
- In-app purchases
- Ads
- Analytics
- User-generated content sharing or publishing. The player name is editable local input.
- Account deletion flow. “Delete career?” removes a local save.
- App Tracking Transparency prompt

## Data and support

Player names, settings, careers and results stay on the device. The widget reads the active career locally, and device backups follow the user’s system settings. The app works offline. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

None.

## Platform

- Native iPhone and iPad support. Minimum iOS and iPadOS version is 17.6.
- iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait. On wider layouts, the match and season overview cards can sit side by side.
- The same fictional clubs, career rules and features are available across regions. The native interface is localized in English, German, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Spanish and Russian, with English as the fallback. Club names and “OneXI Career” are shared across languages. Date, time and rating formatting follow system locale conventions.
