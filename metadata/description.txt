Create a fictional footballer and build a career over successive seasons. Choose your shirt number and play as a striker, midfielder or defender. Start at Lumen Academy and work your way towards Summit United.

Choose a balanced, attacking or defensive approach before each match. The match resolves instantly on your device, with a written report showing the score, your rating, goals, assists and key events. Follow the league table and home and away fixtures across a 14-round season.

Spend practice points on finishing, passing, pace, defending or stamina. Recover between matches to restore fitness. At the end of the season, you can move up a tier if you meet the next club’s requirements for overall rating, career matches and reputation. You can also stay and start another season at your current club.

Track career totals, milestones, trophies and past seasons. Keep up to four local saves, see your active career in a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget and set optional reminders.

OneXI Career is an offline, single-player game for iPhone and iPad. The game interface is available in English, German, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Spanish and Russian.
