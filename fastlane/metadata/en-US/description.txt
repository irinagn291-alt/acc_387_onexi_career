Create a fictional soccer player and build a career one season at a time. Pick a jersey number and play as a striker, midfielder, or defender. Start at Lumen Academy and work your way toward Summit United.

Set a balanced, attacking, or defensive approach before each match. Matches resolve instantly on your device. Read the written report for the score, your rating, goals, assists, and key events. Follow your team's place in the standings and check the home and away schedule across all 14 rounds.

Use practice points to improve finishing, passing, pace, defending, or stamina. Recover between matches to restore fitness. After the final round, move up a tier if you meet the next club's requirements for overall rating, career matches, and reputation, or stay at your current club for another season.

Track career totals, milestones, trophies, and past seasons. Keep up to four local saves, check your active career from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, and set optional reminders.

OneXI Career is an offline, single-player game for iPhone and iPad. The game interface is available in English, German, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Spanish, and Russian.
