Version 1.2.1, build 2127. The app supports iPhone and iPad on iOS or iPadOS 17.6 or later. A fresh production install opens New career. Enter a player name, choose a shirt number and position, then begin. No sign-in or internet connection is required.

In Career, select a match approach and simulate a fixture. The app resolves it immediately and opens a written report with the score, player rating and match events. Close the report to check Season Pulse, the league table, fixtures and the Match Journal. Use Training to spend practice points on a skill and try recovery between matches. Complete 14 rounds to see the season summary. In Clubs, stay for another season or confirm a move if all three requirements are met. Legacy displays career totals, milestones, earned trophies and completed seasons. Settings supports up to four local careers and lets you switch or delete saves. Relaunching restores the active career.

Reminders are off by default. Enable them in Settings to trigger the system request for notification alerts and sound. If allowed, the app schedules local reminders for the next match and estimated season finish in the active unfinished career. Denying permission leaves gameplay available. Reminders neither gate play nor advance the simulation. The project declares a push entitlement and remote-notification background mode. Delivery is local, with no APNs registration or remote push handling.

Save data is written as local JSON in Application Support and copied locally to an App Group for WidgetKit. Add a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget to see the active career. Tapping it opens Career through clubcareer94://career. There are no accounts, network gameplay, analytics, ads, purchases or Live Activities. The privacy manifest declares no tracking or collected data types. Backups follow system settings.
